What a successful year looks like 06

~15% occupancy is what success looks like.

A high-performing Cocoon property typically operates at ~15% calendar occupancy across the year — roughly 55 days out of 365.

That distribution is bursty. A given month might have zero bookings; another might run a three-day production block plus two evening events. Revenue is annualized — not every month hits the average, and several months will materially exceed it.

At 21 Astor's flagship-tier rates, 15% occupancy on Productions alone at Tier 2 headcount (15–24 crew, $1,945/hr, 8-hr day) returns ~$80,500 per year to you at the 50% split — before any Showroom or Corporate bookings stack on top.

We monitor the curve monthly and report with full transparency. If inquiry volume is strong, we look at lifting rates. If conversion is soft, we look at the tier structure. Nothing moves without your approval.
